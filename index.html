Questions

Frequently asked

Do I need to know how to code to join?

No. Curiosity is the only requirement. We have beginners and experts, and beginner-friendly tracks in each pillar.

Is there a membership fee?

Creating an account is free. After you sign up you can stay on the free read-only membership, or choose a paid Annual or Lifetime plan from your dashboard — see what each includes.

How much time does it take?

Most members spend 2–4 hours a week. Research tracks need a bit more; discussions and webinars are drop-in.

Can I attend webinars online?

Yes. All webinars are live online with recordings available afterwards for members.
